Game Features

Tap Frenzy+ is packed with features designed to keep you engaged, challenged, and coming back every day. Every session is a new opportunity to beat your personal record.

🎯
Daily Targets
Yesterday's best score becomes today's target. Beat it to celebrate and set a new challenge for tomorrow. Every mode (15s, 30s, 1min etc.) has its own independent target.
🔥
Respect+ Streak
Play every day to build your 30-day Respect+ streak. Watch your bar go from red to orange to legendary green. Missing a day resets it — so stay consistent!
⚡
Combo System
Hit targets consecutively to build your combo multiplier — up to 16x! Each combo level plays a higher musical note. Reach x5, x10, and x16 for special milestone fanfares.
🎮
4 Power-Ups
❄️ Freeze Time, ⚡ 2x Points, ☄️ Clear Field, and 💣 Bomb (a trap!). Power-ups appear randomly on the field — tap wisely to gain advantages or avoid punishment.
📅
Weekly Scoreboard
Track your scores for every day of the current week, organized by game mode. The board resets every Sunday — can you dominate all 7 days?
🎵
Procedural Music
All music and sound effects are generated in real-time using the Web Audio API — no audio files. The beat shifts to urgent mode when time runs low. Toggle music and SFX independently.
⏱️
11 Time Modes
From a quick 15-second burst to a full 5-minute endurance challenge. Choose your preferred duration and compete in that category's scoreboard independently.
📈
Leveling System
Earn XP with every hit and level up during your run. Higher levels mean targets appear faster and the game gets progressively more challenging — can you reach level 10?

How to Play

Tap Frenzy+ is easy to pick up but hard to master. Here's everything you need to know to start scoring high from your very first game.

01
Choose your duration. Select how long you want to play — from a quick 15-second sprint to a challenging 5-minute marathon. Each duration has its own daily target and weekly scoreboard.
02
Tap the targets. Colourful circular targets appear on screen with a shrinking ring showing how long they last. Tap them before the ring disappears or you'll miss — and lose your combo.
03
Build your combo. Every successful tap increases your combo multiplier. Your score per target multiplies by your current combo — so a x10 combo turns a 10-point target into 100 points!
04
Use power-ups wisely. Special power-up tiles appear randomly. ❄️ Freeze stops the clock for 4 seconds. ⚡ doubles all points for 5 seconds. ☄️ clears the screen for bonus points. 💣 is a trap — it resets your combo!
05
Beat your daily target. Yesterday's best score for this mode is your target today. Beat it to trigger a celebration, earn a new Respect+ streak day, and set a new target for tomorrow.
06
Build your Respect+ streak. Play at least once every day to maintain your streak. After 26 days your bar turns green — legendary status. Miss one day and it resets. Consistency is the real game.

About Tap Frenzy+

Tap Frenzy+ is a free, browser-based reaction speed game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no plugins, no downloads required. It runs entirely in your browser on any device.

The game was designed to be genuinely engaging with a loop that rewards daily play: a combo system that feels satisfying, a daily target that gives you a reason to come back, and a 30-day streak challenge that builds real habit. All music and sound effects are procedurally generated using the Web Audio API, meaning zero audio files are needed.

Tap Frenzy+ is free to play and supported by advertising via Google AdSense. No personal data is collected beyond what is stored locally in your browser (your scores and streak). Nothing is sent to any server.
